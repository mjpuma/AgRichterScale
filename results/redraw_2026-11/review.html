Caption

Event points are SPAM 2020 harvested area and production of the selected crops (wheat, maize, rice, or all grain) inside the places listed for each event in the disruption workbooks. A country flagged at country level contributes its whole area; a country flagged at state level contributes only its own listed subdivisions. All of that harvest is counted as disrupted, an implicit loss fraction of 1. The points are 2020 exposure under complete disruption, not observed historical losses. A place that could not be mapped adds zero to its event and is recorded as a mapping failure; that zero is not a measured harvest. The 23 workbook events are plotted; the four registry extensions without a workbook sheet are not.
